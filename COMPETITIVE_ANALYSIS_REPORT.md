## Part 2: Industry Best Practices - Top-Performing Glass Companies (National)

Research into nationally top-performing glass service companies reveals these winning patterns:

### Best Practice #1: Service Page Architecture
**High-performing companies structure service pages with:**

1. **Keyword-optimized H1** (e.g., "Auto Glass Replacement in San Antonio")
2. **High-citability intro block** (3-4 sentences answering "What is this?" and "Why does it matter?")
3. **Problem/Solution structure** (mirrors voice-search query patterns)
4. **Embedded FAQ section** (10-15 questions with rich snippets)
5. **Process steps** (with HowTo schema markup)
6. **Pricing/options table** (crystal clear)
7. **Before/after gallery** (with alt text and descriptions)
8. **Trust signals** (certifications, warranties, guarantees)
9. **Local service area map** (with schema markup)
10. **CTA buttons** (multiple, above and below fold)

**Example Template:**
```
H1: "Windshield Repair & Replacement in San Antonio, TX"

Intro Block:
"A cracked windshield isn't just a safety issue—it's a legal one in Texas. 
Master Glass Solutions offers same-day windshield repair and replacement

your vehicle to factory specifications."

Why Choose Section:
- 24/7 emergency service availability
- Mobile unit comes to you

- Lifetime warranty on materials

Process Section (with HowTo schema):
1. Free damage assessment (online or in-person)
2. Quote provided in minutes
3. Technician arrives 2-4 hours or scheduled
4. Professional installation (typically 1 hour)
5. Full curing time (1-4 hours per type)

FAQ Section (with FAQ schema):
Q: How long does windshield replacement take?
A: Most replacements take 1-2 hours from start to finish...

Pricing Table:
| Service | Typical Cost | Notes |

```
