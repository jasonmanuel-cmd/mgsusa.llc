## 💡 Pro Tips

### Finding Quality Reviews
- ⭐ Pick reviews that mention **specific services** ("window repair," "emergency glass")
- ⭐ Pick **recent reviews** (last 3 months = more credible)
- ⭐ Mix of **5-star ratings** (shows you have consistent quality)
- ❌ Skip generic praise ("great company!") - be specific

### Finding Quality Videos
- 🎬 **Professional quality** (sharp, good lighting)
- 🎬 **Action-oriented** (installation, repair, before/after)
- 🎬 **Recent** (July 2026 is better than January 2026)
- 🎬 **Authentic** (real work, not overly polished)

## 🚨 Troubleshooting

### "I can't find a Reviews section on Facebook"
- Try scrolling down the page
- Look for "Recommendations" tab
- Check if reviews are enabled on the business page

### "The Instagram video URL is really long"
- That's normal! Copy the whole thing
- Instagram will simplify it automatically
- Just make sure you have `/p/` in the URL

### "I found great reviews but they're old (from 2024)"
- Recent reviews (last 3 months) are better for freshness
- But old positive reviews still help if that's all you have
- Collect what's available
