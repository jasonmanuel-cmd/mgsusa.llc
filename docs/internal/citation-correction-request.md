**Listing:** `sanantonioemergencyhomeservices.com/listings/master-glass-solutions/`
**Platform:** Operative / youroptive.com
**Found:** 2026-09-10 audit · **Drafted:** 2026-10-06

## Why this one matters

Three of the four errors are ordinary NAP noise. One is not:

> **Website shown: `www.infinityglasscompany.com`**

That is a different glass company. Anyone who finds Master Glass Solutions on
that directory and clicks through lands on a competitor. The phone number is
wrong too, so the two ways a customer could reach the business both fail, and
the listing carries a "VERIFIED" badge that tells them not to doubt it.

Lead that fact when asking. A directory will shrug at a wrong ZIP; a listing
that misdirects customers to another business is something they have an
interest in fixing.

## If there is no reply

1. **Two weeks, then follow up once**, replying to the original so the thread
   holds the history.
2. **Try the platform rather than the site.** The directory runs on
   Operative/youroptive.com; their support can act on listings their customers
   publish.
3. **Escalate on the competitor link, not the SEO.** A verified listing
   directing customers to another business is the part a host will act on.
4. **Leave it if it never moves.** One bad citation on a low-authority
   directory is a small, slow negative. It is not worth paying a citation
   cleanup service over, and it is not what is holding the lead volume down —
   that is traffic.

The audit is from 2026-09-10 and the listing was not re-checked since; this
environment blocks the host, so it could not be verified from here. **Open the
