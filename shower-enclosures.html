What is a frameless shower enclosure?+

A frameless shower enclosure uses heavy glass and minimal visible framing to create a clean, open visual effect. The correct configuration depends on the room and opening.

What do I need before requesting a shower glass quote?+

Send photos of the shower, rough dimensions if available, your desired door or panel style, property location, and the stage of the remodel.

Can you help with a custom shower glass layout?+

Yes. A custom shower enclosure path is appropriate when the opening, design, or hardware needs to be considered around the room.
