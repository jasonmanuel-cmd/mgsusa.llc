AT A GLANCE

What is security window film?

Security window film is a thick optically clear polyester laminate bonded to the interior face of glass you already have. The glass still breaks. The film keeps the fragments bonded to one another and, where an attachment system is used, keeps the whole pane anchored to the frame.

It is installed on existing glazing, so it is usually the least disruptive option for an occupied building — no frame work, no glass order, no opening left unsecured overnight. We install on commercial buildings only: storefronts, offices, clinics, schools and industrial properties. No vehicle glass of any kind.

WHEN YOU MAY NEED THIS

Start here when…

  • Your storefront or ground-floor glass has been hit before, or sits somewhere that invites it.
  • You want to slow a smash-and-grab without bars, roll shutters or replacing the glazing.
  • A tenant agreement, insurer or corporate standard calls for glass retention.
  • You need fragment containment in an occupied space — lobby, clinic, classroom or office.

What influences the investment?

Security film cost is driven more by thickness and anchoring than by area. A 4 mil safety film and a 14 mil film with a wet-glaze attachment system are different products doing different jobs, at different prices.

Pane count and size, working height, frame condition, and whether the glass is annealed, tempered or insulated all move the number. Share photos and rough dimensions and the first conversation can be specific rather than theoretical.

Does security film make glass bulletproof?+

No. Security film is not ballistic glazing and should never be sold as it. It is a delay and containment product: the glass still breaks, but it stays together and stays in the frame, which turns a quick smash-and-grab into a slow, loud, visible effort that most people abandon. If you need genuine ballistic protection, that is a different product and a different budget, and we will say so.

Do you install security film on vehicles?+

No. Master Glass Solutions works on buildings only — commercial and residential property. We do not install film on cars, trucks, windshields or any vehicle glass, and we do not offer mobile auto service.

How long does security film last?+

Interior-applied film on commercial glazing is generally expected to last well over a decade, and manufacturers warrant it in that range. Exposure, orientation and glass type all matter. Failure usually shows as edge lift or haze long before the film stops doing its job.
