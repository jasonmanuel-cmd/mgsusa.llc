AT A GLANCE

What is perforated window film?

Perforated window film — often called one-way vision film — is a printed vinyl punched through with a regular pattern of small holes, typically around half the surface area. From outside, where it is bright, the eye reads the printed graphic. From inside, where it is darker, the eye reads straight through the holes.

It is how a storefront can carry full-window advertising without blinding the staff or hiding the shop. Applied to commercial building glass only — storefronts, offices and retail frontage. Not vehicle glass.

What influences the investment?

Perforated graphics cost is mostly print area and install complexity. Coverage, the number of separate panes, and whether existing film has to come off first are the main drivers.

Artwork that already exists at the right size costs less than artwork that has to be built. Send the storefront photos and the rough dimensions, and say whether you have print-ready files.

Can people see in through perforated window film?+

During the day, very little — the exterior is brighter, so the printed surface dominates and the holes read as part of the image. After dark the relationship reverses: with interior lights on and the street dark, the film becomes much easier to see through from outside. It is a daytime privacy product, not a blackout, and anyone who tells you otherwise has not stood outside one at night.

Will it damage the glass or the frames?+

No. It is applied to the interior or exterior face with a pressure-sensitive adhesive and is removable. Seasonal and promotional graphics come down without harming properly maintained glazing. Very old or already-damaged film can leave adhesive behind, which is a cleaning job rather than a glass one.

Do you handle the printed artwork?+

We measure the glass and coordinate the print to those measurements, which is the step that stops a graphic arriving at the wrong size for the opening it has to fit. If you have print-ready files, send them with the enquiry. If you do not, say so and we will talk through what is needed before anything goes to print.
