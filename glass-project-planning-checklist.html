WHAT’S INSIDE

What to document before you call.

  1. 01

    Opening + condition

    Capture the width, height, surrounding surfaces, existing damage, and door or window function.

  2. 02

    Visual direction

    Save a few references and note the look, privacy, hardware, or use needs you care about.

  3. 03

    Project constraints

    Write down timing, access, occupancy, business operations, and any renovation dependencies.

    Questions for the glass team

    Use a short prompt list so every estimate conversation begins with clarity.
