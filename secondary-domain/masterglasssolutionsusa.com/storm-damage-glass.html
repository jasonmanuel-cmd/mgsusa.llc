AT A GLANCE

Storm damage to glass is not something to wait on.

A cracked pane can fail suddenly. A broken storefront is a security and weather-exposure problem from the moment it breaks. Call 210-370-3700 as soon as the damage is safe to assess. For occupied buildings, the crew either replaces the glass or boards up the opening the same day.

WHEN TO CALL

When to start here.

  • Hail has cracked, pitted, or shattered window, storefront, or shower glass.
  • Wind or a fallen branch has broken a pane, door glass, or sidelite.
  • Flying debris from a storm has damaged a glass opening.
  • A window or storefront that was fine before a storm now shows a crack, chip, or seal failure.
  • You need documentation and an invoice for an insurance claim on storm-damaged glass.

HOW IT WORKS

A clearer next step.

  1. 01

    Call or submit the details

    Tell us the property address, the damaged opening, the type of storm damage, and whether the situation is urgent. For safety or weather-exposure issues, call 210-370-3700 directly.

  2. 02

    Secure the opening

    If the glass is broken or at risk of failure, the crew either replaces it or boards up the opening to protect the building until replacement glass is ready.

  3. 03

    Document and replace

    We document the damage for your insurance claim, confirm the correct glass type and size, and install the replacement. Same-day response is typical for San Antonio metro properties.

See every question
Does homeowner's insurance cover storm-damaged glass?+

In most cases, yes — hail damage, wind damage, and impact damage from storm debris are covered by standard homeowner's insurance when the damage is to the building's glass. Master Glass Solutions provides a detailed invoice, damage photos, and the glass specification to support your claim. We work with most major homeowner's insurance carriers. Call 210-370-3700 and we will help you understand your coverage.

What should I do immediately after storm damage to glass?+

Call 210-370-3700. If the glass is broken, keep people away from the opening until the crew arrives. If weather or security is a concern, the crew will board up the opening the same day. For cracks that have not yet failed, we still recommend a prompt inspection — a crack can spread and a pane can fail without warning after impact.

How fast can you respond to a storm-damage call?+

For properties in the San Antonio metro area, we typically respond within about an hour of the call during and after storm events. Response times for outlying Hill Country communities may be longer depending on road conditions and distance. Call 210-370-3700 for an estimated response time to your location.

Do you handle the insurance paperwork?+

We provide the documentation your insurance company needs — a detailed invoice, photos of the damage, and the glass specification. The claim itself is between you and your carrier, but Master Glass Solutions makes the documentation part straightforward. If you have a claim number, share it when you call or submit the quote form.

Is emergency board-up covered by insurance?+

Emergency board-up is often covered as a mitigation expense under homeowner's and commercial property policies — the idea being that securing the opening prevents additional damage. We invoice board-up separately so you can submit it as part of your claim. Call 210-370-3700 to discuss the situation and get a board-up arranged the same day.

Do you work on vehicles or windshields after a storm?+

No. Master Glass Solutions is a building glass contractor. We do not service auto glass, windshields, or any vehicle glass. Storm damage to a vehicle windshield should go to an auto glass shop. Master Glass Solutions handles broken glass in homes and buildings only.
