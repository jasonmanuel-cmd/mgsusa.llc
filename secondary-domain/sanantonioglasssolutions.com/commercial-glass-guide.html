AT A GLANCE

The right commercial glass starts with the opening and the use case.

A storefront needs to be clean, visible, and durable. An entry door needs to be barrier-free and secure. An interior partition needs to be the right sightline and operate smoothly day to day. A patient-room door in a clinic needs to be wide, low-threshold, and reliable. Master Glass Solutions matches the glass and hardware to the building, not the other way around.

What influences commercial glass cost?

Commercial glass cost is shaped by the opening, the glass specification, the hardware, the framing condition, and the schedule.

A single storefront pane in an existing frame costs less than a full storefront system with new doors, sidelites, and hardware. A barrier-free patient-room sliding door with fixed sidelites costs more than a standard interior partition because of the width, the hardware, and the ADA requirements. Site access, after-hours scheduling, and any urgent securing needs also affect the quote. A photo, rough dimensions, and a description of the opening are enough to start.

What types of commercial glass do you install?+

Master Glass Solutions installs storefront glass systems, entry doors, interior partitions, architectural glazing, and healthcare glazing such as patient-room sliding doors. Glass options include tempered, laminated, insulated, and low-E-coated glass. All commercial work meets applicable building-code and safety requirements.

Do you handle ADA-compliant glass openings?+

Yes. ADA considerations affect entry doors, patient-room doors, and some interior partitions — things like clear width, threshold height, hardware operation, and swing. Master Glass Solutions installs commercial glass openings that meet ADA requirements, including sliding glass door systems with fixed sidelites for patient rooms in medical facilities.

How long does a commercial glass installation take?+

It depends on the scope. A single storefront pane or door glass replacement may be completed in a single visit. A new storefront system with doors, sidelites, and hardware takes longer and is scheduled around your business hours. For multi-opening or architectural work, we provide a timeline with the estimate. Call 210-370-3700 with the project details for a schedule estimate.

Can you replace glass in an existing storefront frame?+

Yes. In many cases we can replace the glass in an existing storefront frame without replacing the whole system — this is common after storm damage, break-ins, or when a pane has failed. We measure the opening, match the glass type and size, and install it in the existing frame. If the framing itself is damaged or the system is beyond repair, we discuss a full replacement.

Do you work with commercial property managers and facility teams?+

Yes. Master Glass Solutions works with property managers, facility teams, and business owners across San Antonio and Boerne. For occupied buildings, we coordinate scheduling around access constraints and operating hours. For multi-site properties, we can scope each opening individually. Call 210-370-3700 or use the commercial quote form.

Is tempered or laminated glass better for storefronts?+

Tempered glass is the standard for storefronts because of its strength and safety properties. Laminated glass adds a plastic interlayer that holds the glass together when broken — useful where security or glass retention matters. Some storefront systems use laminated glass for the door and tempered glass for the sidelites and display panels. We match the specification to the opening and the risk profile.
