WHAT WE SEE HERE

Glass that has to stay open for business.

The retail corridor along I-35 at Olympia Parkway means storefront systems, entrance doors, interior partitions and a steady rhythm of tenants moving in and out. Customer-facing glass is specified differently from the glass in a house: tempered or laminated for safety and security, and a door that has to work several hundred times a day without complaint.

The constraint is rarely the glass. It is that the unit is trading. Work gets sequenced around opening hours, deliveries and the centre’s own rules about when contractors can be on site, and that gets agreed before anyone turns up with a van.

TENANT CHANGEOVER

Between one sign and the next.

When a unit changes hands the glass usually changes with it — old vinyl and perforated graphics off, new film or graphics on, sometimes a different door configuration or a partition moved. That window between leases is short and everything in it is on the critical path to opening.

Scoping it early is what keeps it off that path. Measuring the frontage while the previous tenant is still in place, ordering to those measurements, and installing in the gap is a far calmer job than discovering on handover that the glass does not suit the new fit-out.

FAQ

Selma glass service FAQs.

See every question
Do you handle commercial storefront glass in Selma?+

Yes, and it is most of what we do there. Storefront systems, entrance doors and closers, interior partitions, tempered and laminated replacement glass, security film, perforated window graphics and 24/7 emergency board-up. We also cover residential work in the surrounding neighbourhoods. Buildings only — no auto glass.

A storefront pane went overnight. What happens first?+

The board-up. Call 210-370-3700 whatever the hour and we secure the opening so the unit is weatherproof and not an invitation, then measure and order the replacement. Getting you trading in the morning and getting the right glass in are two different jobs, and trying to do them as one is what leaves a shop shut for a day.

Can glass work be done outside trading hours?+

Yes, and for an occupied retail unit it is usually the right answer. Tell us the centre’s contractor access rules and your own opening hours when you ask for a quote — those shape the sequence and the price more than the glass itself does.

Do you fit window graphics and film for a new tenant?+

Yes. Perforated one-way vision graphics advertise to the street while keeping the view out from inside, and solar control film cuts heat and glare through the glass already in the opening. Both go on commercial buildings here — never vehicles. Removal of the previous tenant’s graphics can be quoted at the same time.
