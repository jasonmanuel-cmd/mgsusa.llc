What types of residential glass work do you provide?+

Residential services include frameless and framed shower enclosures, custom-cut mirrors for walls and vanities, window glass replacement for existing openings, glass table tops, and other custom home glass. Broken residential glass is also covered by the 24/7 emergency line. Send photos, the rough dimensions, and the property address to 210-370-3700 or use the quote form, and the team will confirm the measurements on site before fabricating.

Can you help with a custom shower enclosure?+

Yes. Custom shower enclosures are built around the actual opening rather than fitted from a stock kit, covering frameless doors, sliding systems, and fixed panels with the hardware finish you want. Share photos of the shower, approximate dimensions, the design direction, and your timing. The team confirms exact measurements on site after tile is set, since finished tile dimensions drive the glass and hardware. Call 210-370-3700 to start.

What should I send for a residential glass quote?+

Start with the property location, service type, photos, rough dimensions if known, and desired timing. The more context you share, the easier it is to guide the next step.
