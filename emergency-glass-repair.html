Do you offer 24/7 emergency glass repair?+

Yes. Master Glass Solutions states 24/7 support for emergency glass needs. Call 210-370-3700 to explain the situation and location.

What should I do first after glass breaks?+

Keep people away from loose glass, avoid touching damaged edges, protect the area if it can be done safely, and call for support. If there is an immediate danger or emergency, contact local emergency services first.

Can you help with a broken storefront window?+

Storefront damage is an appropriate reason to call the emergency support line. Explain the type of opening, property location, whether it is secure, and any immediate safety concern.
