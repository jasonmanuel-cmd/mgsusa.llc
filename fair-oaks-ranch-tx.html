WHAT WE SEE HERE

Windows old enough to have failed.

Double-pane units are two sheets of glass with a sealed cavity between them. The seal has a service life, and on houses built from the 1970s through the 1990s a good number of them have reached it. When a seal fails, moisture gets into the cavity and the unit fogs or streaks from the inside — where no amount of cleaning will ever reach it.

The frame is usually fine. It is the sealed unit inside it that needs replacing, which is a far smaller job than replacing the window, and the one people most often assume they cannot have done separately. If a pane looks permanently misty or has tide marks between the glass, that is what you are looking at.

GOLF COURSE FRONTAGE

Glass that gets hit.

Properties backing onto the course take the occasional ball, and a struck pane does not always break on the spot. Tempered glass can carry a chip or an impact point for a long time before it finally goes, and when it does go it goes all at once.

Worth getting a look at rather than leaving. The same applies to the large rear elevations facing the fairway, where the glass is both the most exposed and the most expensive to replace. Mature oak cover is the other half of it — limbs come down in a storm and land on exactly that glass.

Do you serve Fair Oaks Ranch?+

Yes. Fair Oaks Ranch sits between our office and Boerne, so it is well inside the area we cover daily. Residential and commercial glass, failed insulated unit replacement, frameless shower enclosures, custom mirrors, glass railings and 24/7 emergency board-up. Buildings only — no auto glass.

My window is foggy between the panes. Can that be fixed?+

The sealed unit has failed and moisture is inside the cavity, so it cannot be cleaned — but in most cases the glass unit can be replaced while the existing frame and sash stay exactly where they are. That is a much smaller and cheaper job than a new window, and it is the one most people are surprised is available. Send a photo and the rough dimensions and we can tell you what is involved.

A golf ball hit my window but it did not break. Does it matter?+

It can. An impact can leave a chip or a stress point that holds for months and then fails without warning, and tempered glass fails completely rather than cracking politely. If the strike is on a large or hard-to-reach pane it is worth having looked at while it is still a scheduled job rather than an emergency one.
